Addition and subtraction of complex numbers in Cartesian form

For complex numbers \(z_1 = a + bi\) and \(z_2 = c + di\):
\[z_1 + z_2 = (a+c) + (b+d)i\]\[z_1 - z_2 = (a-c) + (b-d)i\]Real parts combine with real parts, and imaginary parts combine with imaginary parts, exactly as with like terms in a binomial.
Worked exampleLet \(z_1 = 7 - 3i\) and \(z_2 = -2 + 5i\). Find \(z_1 + z_2\) and \(z_1 - z_2\).
\[z_1 + z_2 = (7 + (-2)) + (-3 + 5)i\]\[\boxed{z_1 + z_2 = 5 + 2i}\]\[z_1 - z_2 = (7 - (-2)) + (-3 - 5)i\]\[\boxed{z_1 - z_2 = 9 - 8i}\]

Multiplication of complex numbers in Cartesian form

For \(z_1 = a + bi\) and \(z_2 = c + di\), expand as a binomial product and use \(i^2 = -1\):
\[z_1 z_2 = (a+bi)(c+di) = ac + adi + bci + bdi^2\]\[\boxed{z_1 z_2 = (ac - bd) + (ad + bc)i}\]
Worked exampleFind \((4 - 5i)(7 + 8i)\).
\[(4 - 5i)(7 + 8i) = 4(7) + 4(8i) - 5i(7) - 5i(8i)\]\[= 28 + 32i - 35i - 40i^2\]\[= 28 - 3i - 40(-1)\]\[\boxed{(4 - 5i)(7 + 8i) = 68 - 3i}\]

Division of complex numbers using the conjugate

To divide by \(c + di\), multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate \(c - di\) of the denominator, so that the denominator becomes real:
\[\frac{a+bi}{c+di} = \frac{(a+bi)(c-di)}{(c+di)(c-di)} = \frac{(ac+bd) + (bc-ad)i}{c^2+d^2}\]\[\boxed{\frac{a+bi}{c+di} = \frac{ac+bd}{c^2+d^2} + \frac{bc-ad}{c^2+d^2}i}\]
Worked exampleFind the quotient \(\dfrac{2+3i}{1+2i}\).
\[\frac{2+3i}{1+2i} = \frac{(2+3i)(1-2i)}{(1+2i)(1-2i)}\]\[= \frac{2 - 4i + 3i - 6i^2}{1^2 + 2^2} = \frac{2 - i + 6}{5}\]\[\boxed{\frac{2+3i}{1+2i} = \frac{8}{5} - \frac{1}{5}i}\]

The modulus of a complex number

For \(z = a + bi\), the modulus (or absolute value) of \(z\) is its distance from the origin in the complex plane:
\[|z| = \sqrt{a^2+b^2}\]Since \(z\overline{z} = (a+bi)(a-bi) = a^2+b^2\), it follows that \(|z| = \sqrt{z\overline{z}}\) and \(|z| = |\overline{z}|\).
Worked exampleCalculate the modulus of \(z_1 = 5 - 6i\) and \(z_2 = 12 + 5i\).
\[|z_1| = \sqrt{5^2 + (-6)^2} = \sqrt{25+36}\]\[\boxed{|z_1| = \sqrt{61}}\]\[|z_2| = \sqrt{12^2+5^2} = \sqrt{144+25} = \sqrt{169}\]\[\boxed{|z_2| = 13}\]

The modulus of a product

For any two complex numbers \(z_1\) and \(z_2\):
\[|z_1 z_2| = |z_1||z_2|\]The modulus of a product equals the product of the moduli.
Worked exampleEvaluate \(|(3+4i)(5+12i)|\).
\[|(3+4i)(5+12i)| = |3+4i|\,|5+12i|\]\[= \sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\sqrt{5^2+12^2} = \sqrt{25}\,\sqrt{169} = 5 \times 13\]\[\boxed{|(3+4i)(5+12i)| = 65}\]
