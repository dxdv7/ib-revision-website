Volume and surface area of a cuboid

For a cuboid (rectangular prism) with length \(l\), width \(w\), and height \(h\):
\[V = lwh\]\[S = 2(lw + lh + wh)\]
Worked exampleFind the volume and surface area of a cuboid with length 6 cm, width 4 cm, and height 5 cm.
\[V = 6 \times 4 \times 5\]\[\boxed{V = 120 \text{ cm}^3}\]\[S = 2(6 \times 4 + 6 \times 5 + 4 \times 5) = 2(24 + 30 + 20)\]\[\boxed{S = 2(74) = 148 \text{ cm}^2}\]

Volume and surface area of a right prism

For a right prism with a polygonal base of area \(A\) and height \(h\) (the perpendicular distance between the two congruent bases):
\[V = Ah\]\[S = 2A + \sum (\text{lateral face areas})\]Each lateral face of a right prism is a rectangle, so for a right prism the sum of the lateral face areas equals the perimeter of the base multiplied by \(h\).
Worked exampleA right prism has a right-angled triangular base with legs 3 cm and 4 cm (hypotenuse 5 cm), and a height of 10 cm. Find the volume and surface area.
\[A = \frac{1}{2}(3)(4) = 6 \text{ cm}^2\]\[V = Ah = 6 \times 10\]\[\boxed{V = 60 \text{ cm}^3}\]The perimeter of the base is \(3 + 4 + 5 = 12\) cm, so the lateral area is \(12 \times 10 = 120 \text{ cm}^2\).
\[S = 2(6) + 120\]\[\boxed{S = 132 \text{ cm}^2}\]

Volume and surface area of a pyramid

For a pyramid with a polygonal base of area \(A_{\text{base}}\) and height \(h\):
\[V = \frac{1}{3} A_{\text{base}} h\]\[S = A_{\text{base}} + \sum (\text{lateral triangular face areas})\]
Worked exampleA right pyramid has a square base of side 6 cm and height 4 cm. Find the volume and surface area.
The slant height from the apex to the midpoint of a base edge is:
\[l = \sqrt{h^2 + \left(\frac{6}{2}\right)^2} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5\]Each triangular lateral face has area \(\frac{1}{2}(6)(5) = 15 \text{ cm}^2\), so the four faces have total area \(60 \text{ cm}^2\).
\[V = \frac{1}{3}(6^2)(4) = \frac{1}{3}(144)\]\[\boxed{V = 48 \text{ cm}^3}\]\[S = 36 + 4(15)\]\[\boxed{S = 96 \text{ cm}^2}\]

Volume and surface area of a sphere

For a sphere of radius \(r\):
\[V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\]\[S = 4\pi r^2\]
Worked exampleFind the exact volume and surface area of a sphere with radius 6 cm.
\[V = \frac{4}{3}\pi (6)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(216)\]\[\boxed{V = 288\pi \text{ cm}^3}\]\[S = 4\pi(6)^2 = 4\pi(36)\]\[\boxed{S = 144\pi \text{ cm}^2}\]

Volume and surface area of a cylinder

For a cylinder of radius \(r\) and height \(h\):
\[V = \pi r^2 h\]\[S = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h = 2\pi r(r + h)\]
Worked exampleFind the exact volume and surface area of a cylinder with radius 3 cm and height 8 cm.
\[V = \pi (3)^2(8)\]\[\boxed{V = 72\pi \text{ cm}^3}\]\[S = 2\pi(3)^2 + 2\pi(3)(8) = 18\pi + 48\pi\]\[\boxed{S = 66\pi \text{ cm}^2}\]

Volume and surface area of a cone

For a cone of radius \(r\) and height \(h\), with slant height \(l\):
\[V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\]\[S = \pi r^2 + \pi r l, \quad \text{where } l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}\]
Worked exampleFind the exact volume and surface area of a cone with radius 3 cm and height 4 cm.
\[l = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5\]\[V = \frac{1}{3}\pi (3)^2(4) = \frac{1}{3}\pi(36)\]\[\boxed{V = 12\pi \text{ cm}^3}\]\[S = \pi(3)^2 + \pi(3)(5) = 9\pi + 15\pi\]\[\boxed{S = 24\pi \text{ cm}^2}\]
