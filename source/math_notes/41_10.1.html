Testing for a geometric sequence and the common ratio

A sequence u1, u2, u3, … is geometric if the ratio between consecutive terms is constant: r = un+1 / un. This constant r is called the common ratio.
Worked exampleShow that the sequence 5, 15, 45, 135, … is geometric and state the common ratio.
15 / 5 = 3
45 / 15 = 3
135 / 45 = 3
The ratio between consecutive terms is constant, so the sequence is geometric with common ratio r = 3.

The nth term formula

un = u1 × rn−1, where u1 is the first term, r is the common ratio, and n is the position of the term in the sequence.
Worked exampleA geometric sequence has first term u1 = 4 and common ratio r = −2. Find u8.
u8 = u1 × r8−1 = 4 × (−2)7
(−2)7 = −128
u8 = 4 × (−128) = −512
Final answer: u8 = −512.

Percentage growth applications of the nth term formula

For a quantity increasing by i% each period, the common ratio is r = 1 + i / 100, and the value after n − 1 periods of growth is un = u1 × rn−1.
Worked exampleCarolien earns EUR 48000 in her first year of work. Her salary increases by 5% every year. Find her salary in her fifth year.
r = 1 + 5 / 100 = 1.05
u1 = 48000, n = 5
u5 = 48000 × 1.054
1.054 = 1.21550625
u5 = 48000 × 1.21550625 = 58344.30
Final answer: her salary in year 5 is EUR 58344.30.

Percentage decay applications of the nth term formula

For a quantity decreasing by i% each period, the common ratio is r = 1 − i / 100. The value after n years of depreciation applied to an initial value u1 is u1 × rn.
Worked exampleMachinery is bought for USD 25000 and depreciates at 8% per annum. Find its value after 5 years.
r = 1 − 8 / 100 = 0.92
Value after 5 years = 25000 × 0.925
0.925 = 0.65908152 (5 d.p. shown)
Value = 25000 × 0.65908152 = 16477.04
Final answer: the machinery is worth USD 16477.04 after 5 years.

Finding u1 and r from two given terms

If two terms ua and ub of a geometric sequence are known, then ub / ua = rb−a. Solve this equation for r, then substitute back to find u1.
Worked exampleThe second term of a geometric sequence is 18 and the fifth term is 486. All terms are positive. Find the common ratio and the first term.
u5 / u2 = r5−2 = r3
486 / 18 = r3
r3 = 27, so r = 3
u2 = u1 × r, so 18 = u1 × 3
u1 = 6
Final answer: r = 3 and u1 = 6.

Sum of the first n terms of a geometric series

Sn = u1(rn − 1) / (r − 1), which can equally be written as Sn = u1(1 − rn) / (1 − r), where u1 is the first term, r is the common ratio (r ≠ 1), and n is the number of terms added.
Worked exampleFind the sum of the first 8 terms of the geometric series 7 + 28 + 112 + 448 + …
u1 = 7, r = 28 / 7 = 4, n = 8
S8 = 7(48 − 1) / (4 − 1)
48 = 65536
S8 = 7(65536 − 1) / 3 = 7 × 65535 / 3 = 7 × 21845
Final answer: S8 = 152915.

Applications of the finite sum formula

To find the total accumulated over n periods when a starting amount u1 grows or shrinks by a fixed percentage each period, first find r, then apply Sn = u1(rn − 1) / (r − 1).
Worked exampleAn investor contributes USD 2000 in year 1. Each following year the contribution increases by 6% on the previous year’s contribution. Find the total amount contributed over 6 years.
u1 = 2000, r = 1.06, n = 6
S6 = 2000(1.066 − 1) / (1.06 − 1)
1.066 = 1.41851911 (5 d.p.)
S6 = 2000(0.41851911) / 0.06 = 2000 × 6.9753185
Final answer: total contributions = USD 13950.64 (2 d.p.).

Sum to infinity of a convergent geometric series

S∞ = u1 / (1 − r), valid only when |r| < 1. If |r| ≥ 1 the series diverges and has no sum to infinity.
Worked exampleFind the sum to infinity of the geometric series with first term u1 = 12 and common ratio r = 1/3.
Check convergence: |1/3| < 1, so the series converges.
S∞ = 12 / (1 − 1/3) = 12 / (2/3)
S∞ = 12 × 3/2
Final answer: S∞ = 18.
