Testing for a geometric sequence and the common ratio

A sequence \(u_1, u_2, u_3, \ldots\) is geometric if the ratio between consecutive terms is constant: \(r = \dfrac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}\). This constant \(r\) is called the common ratio.
Worked exampleShow that the sequence \(5, 15, 45, 135, \ldots\) is geometric and state the common ratio.
\[\frac{15}{5} = 3\]\[\frac{45}{15} = 3\]\[\frac{135}{45} = 3\]The ratio between consecutive terms is constant, so the sequence is geometric with common ratio \(\boxed{r = 3}\).

The nth term formula

\[u_n = u_1 \times r^{n-1}\]where \(u_1\) is the first term, \(r\) is the common ratio, and \(n\) is the position of the term in the sequence.
Worked exampleA geometric sequence has first term \(u_1 = 4\) and common ratio \(r = -2\). Find \(u_8\).
\[u_8 = u_1 \times r^{8-1} = 4 \times (-2)^7\]\[(-2)^7 = -128\]\[u_8 = 4 \times (-128) = -512\]Final answer: \(\boxed{u_8 = -512}\).

Percentage growth applications of the nth term formula

For a quantity increasing by \(i\%\) each period, the common ratio is \(r = 1 + \dfrac{i}{100}\), and the value after \(n - 1\) periods of growth is \[u_n = u_1 \times r^{n-1}\]
Worked exampleCarolien earns EUR 48000 in her first year of work. Her salary increases by 5% every year. Find her salary in her fifth year.
\[r = 1 + \frac{5}{100} = 1.05\]\(u_1 = 48000\), \(n = 5\)
\[u_5 = 48000 \times 1.05^4\]\[1.05^4 = 1.21550625\]\[\boxed{u_5 = 48000 \times 1.21550625 = 58344.30}\]Final answer: her salary in year 5 is EUR 58344.30.

Percentage decay applications of the nth term formula

For a quantity decreasing by \(i\%\) each period, the common ratio is \(r = 1 - \dfrac{i}{100}\). The value after \(n\) years of depreciation applied to an initial value \(u_1\) is \[u_1 \times r^n\]
Worked exampleMachinery is bought for USD 25000 and depreciates at 8% per annum. Find its value after 5 years.
\[r = 1 - \frac{8}{100} = 0.92\]\[\text{Value after 5 years} = 25000 \times 0.92^5\]\[0.92^5 = 0.65908152 \text{ (5 d.p. shown)}\]\[\boxed{\text{Value} = 25000 \times 0.65908152 = 16477.04}\]Final answer: the machinery is worth USD 16477.04 after 5 years.

Finding u1 and r from two given terms

If two terms \(u_a\) and \(u_b\) of a geometric sequence are known, then \[\frac{u_b}{u_a} = r^{b-a}\]Solve this equation for \(r\), then substitute back to find \(u_1\).
Worked exampleThe second term of a geometric sequence is 18 and the fifth term is 486. All terms are positive. Find the common ratio and the first term.
\[\frac{u_5}{u_2} = r^{5-2} = r^3\]\[\frac{486}{18} = r^3\]\[r^3 = 27, \text{ so } \boxed{r = 3}\]\[u_2 = u_1 \times r, \text{ so } 18 = u_1 \times 3\]\[\boxed{u_1 = 6}\]Final answer: \(r = 3\) and \(u_1 = 6\).

Sum of the first n terms of a geometric series

\[S_n = \frac{u_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}\]which can equally be written as \[S_n = \frac{u_1(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}\]where \(u_1\) is the first term, \(r\) is the common ratio (\(r \ne 1\)), and \(n\) is the number of terms added.
Worked exampleFind the sum of the first 8 terms of the geometric series \(7 + 28 + 112 + 448 + \ldots\)
\(u_1 = 7\), \(r = \dfrac{28}{7} = 4\), \(n = 8\)
\[S_8 = \frac{7(4^8 - 1)}{4 - 1}\]\[4^8 = 65536\]\[S_8 = \frac{7(65536 - 1)}{3} = \frac{7 \times 65535}{3} = 7 \times 21845\]Final answer: \(\boxed{S_8 = 152915}\).

Applications of the finite sum formula

To find the total accumulated over \(n\) periods when a starting amount \(u_1\) grows or shrinks by a fixed percentage each period, first find \(r\), then apply \[S_n = \frac{u_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}\]
Worked exampleAn investor contributes USD 2000 in year 1. Each following year the contribution increases by 6% on the previous year’s contribution. Find the total amount contributed over 6 years.
\(u_1 = 2000\), \(r = 1.06\), \(n = 6\)
\[S_6 = \frac{2000(1.06^6 - 1)}{1.06 - 1}\]\[1.06^6 = 1.41851911 \text{ (5 d.p.)}\]\[S_6 = \frac{2000(0.41851911)}{0.06} = 2000 \times 6.9753185\]Final answer: total contributions = \(\boxed{\text{USD } 13950.64 \text{ (2 d.p.)}}\).

Sum to infinity of a convergent geometric series

\[S_{\infty} = \frac{u_1}{1 - r}\]valid only when \(|r| < 1\). If \(|r| \ge 1\) the series diverges and has no sum to infinity.
Worked exampleFind the sum to infinity of the geometric series with first term \(u_1 = 12\) and common ratio \(r = \dfrac{1}{3}\).
Check convergence: \(\left|\dfrac{1}{3}\right| < 1\), so the series converges.
\[S_{\infty} = \frac{12}{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{12}{\frac{2}{3}}\]\[S_{\infty} = 12 \times \frac{3}{2}\]Final answer: \(\boxed{S_{\infty} = 18}\).
