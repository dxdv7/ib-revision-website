Compound interest (annual)

FV = PV × (1 + r/100)n, where FV is the future value, PV is the present value (the initial investment), r is the annual interest rate (%), and n is the number of years, compounded once per year.
Worked examplePaul invests AUD 2000 at 2.9% per annum, compounded annually, for 10 years. Find the future value.
FV = 2000 × (1 + 2.9/100)10
FV = 2000 × (1.029)10
FV = 2000 × 1.330926
FV = AUD 2661.85 (2 dp)

Compound interest (compounded k times per year)

FV = PV × (1 + r/(100k))nk, where k is the number of compounding periods per year (k = 1 yearly, k = 2 half-yearly, k = 4 quarterly, k = 12 monthly), r is the annual interest rate (%), and n is the time in years.
Worked exampleRik invests SGD 40 000 at 5% per annum, compounded half-yearly, for 4 years. Find the future value.
FV = 40 000 × (1 + 5/(100 × 2))4 × 2
FV = 40 000 × (1.025)8
FV = 40 000 × 1.218403
FV = SGD 48 736.12 (2 dp)

Future value of an annuity

FV = A × [(1 + r/100)n − 1] / (r/100), where FV is the future value of the annuity, A is the fixed amount saved at the end of each year, r is the annual interest rate (%), and n is the number of years.
Worked exampleUS$2000 is saved into an annuity at the end of every year for 5 years, at 6% annual interest. Find the future value.
FV = 2000 × [(1 + 6/100)5 − 1] / (6/100)
FV = 2000 × [(1.06)5 − 1] / 0.06
FV = 2000 × [1.338226 − 1] / 0.06
FV = 2000 × 5.637093
FV = US$11 274.19 (2 dp)

Amortization (loan repayment)

P = PV × (r/100) × (1 + r/100)n / [(1 + r/100)n − 1], where P is the repayment made each period, PV is the amount borrowed, r is the interest rate per period (%), and n is the number of periods.
Worked exampleTejas takes out a loan of €35 000 for a car. The loan is for 10 years at 1% interest per month. Find the monthly repayment.
n = 10 × 12 = 120 months
P = 35 000 × (1/100) × (1.01)120 / [(1.01)120 − 1]
P = 35 000 × 0.01 × 3.300387 / (3.300387 − 1)
P = 1155.14 / 2.300387
P = €502.15 per month (2 dp)
