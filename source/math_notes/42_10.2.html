Compound interest (annual)

\[FV = PV \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n\]where \(FV\) is the future value, \(PV\) is the present value (the initial investment), \(r\) is the annual interest rate (\%), and \(n\) is the number of years, compounded once per year.
Worked examplePaul invests AUD 2000 at 2.9% per annum, compounded annually, for 10 years. Find the future value.
\[FV = 2000 \times \left(1 + \frac{2.9}{100}\right)^{10}\]\[FV = 2000 \times (1.029)^{10}\]\[FV = 2000 \times 1.330926\]\[\boxed{FV = \text{AUD } 2661.85 \text{ (2 dp)}}\]

Compound interest (compounded k times per year)

\[FV = PV \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{nk}\]where \(k\) is the number of compounding periods per year (\(k = 1\) yearly, \(k = 2\) half-yearly, \(k = 4\) quarterly, \(k = 12\) monthly), \(r\) is the annual interest rate (\%), and \(n\) is the time in years.
Worked exampleRik invests SGD 40000 at 5% per annum, compounded half-yearly, for 4 years. Find the future value.
\[FV = 40000 \times \left(1 + \frac{5}{100 \times 2}\right)^{4 \times 2}\]\[FV = 40000 \times (1.025)^8\]\[FV = 40000 \times 1.218403\]\[\boxed{FV = \text{SGD } 48736.12 \text{ (2 dp)}}\]

Future value of an annuity

\[FV = A \times \frac{\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n - 1}{\frac{r}{100}}\]where \(FV\) is the future value of the annuity, \(A\) is the fixed amount saved at the end of each year, \(r\) is the annual interest rate (\%), and \(n\) is the number of years.
Worked exampleUS$2000 is saved into an annuity at the end of every year for 5 years, at 6% annual interest. Find the future value.
\[FV = 2000 \times \frac{\left(1 + \frac{6}{100}\right)^5 - 1}{\frac{6}{100}}\]\[FV = 2000 \times \frac{(1.06)^5 - 1}{0.06}\]\[FV = 2000 \times \frac{1.338226 - 1}{0.06}\]\[FV = 2000 \times 5.637093\]\[\boxed{FV = \text{USD } 11274.19 \text{ (2 dp)}}\]

Amortization (loan repayment)

\[P = PV \times \frac{r}{100} \times \frac{\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n}{\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n - 1}\]where \(P\) is the repayment made each period, \(PV\) is the amount borrowed, \(r\) is the interest rate per period (\%), and \(n\) is the number of periods.
Worked exampleTejas takes out a loan of €35 000 for a car. The loan is for 10 years at 1% interest per month. Find the monthly repayment.
\[n = 10 \times 12 = 120 \text{ months}\]\[P = 35000 \times \frac{1}{100} \times \frac{(1.01)^{120}}{(1.01)^{120} - 1}\]\[P = 35000 \times 0.01 \times \frac{3.300387}{3.300387 - 1}\]\[P = \frac{1155.14}{2.300387}\]\[\boxed{P = \text{EUR } 502.15 \text{ per month (2 dp)}}\]
