Power rule for integration

\(\displaystyle\int ax^n \, dx = \frac{ax^{n+1}}{n+1} + C\), where \(a\) and \(n\) are constants, \(a \ne 0\), \(n \ne -1\), and \(C\) is the constant of integration.
Worked examplea Find \(\displaystyle\int 6x^2 \, dx\).
\[\int 6x^2 \, dx = \frac{6x^3}{3} + C\]\[\boxed{\int 6x^2 \, dx = 2x^3 + C}\]b Find \(\displaystyle\int 4x^{-3} \, dx\).
\[\int 4x^{-3} \, dx = \frac{4x^{-2}}{-2} + C = -2x^{-2} + C\]\[\boxed{\int 4x^{-3} \, dx = -\frac{2}{x^2} + C}\]

Integrating a sum of terms

\(\displaystyle\int [f(x) + g(x)] \, dx = \int f(x) \, dx + \int g(x) \, dx\). Integrate each term separately using the power rule, then add a single constant of integration, \(C\).
Worked exampleFind \(\displaystyle\int (3x^2 - 4x + 5) \, dx\).
\[\int 3x^2 \, dx = x^3\]\[\int -4x \, dx = -2x^2\]\[\int 5 \, dx = 5x\]\[\boxed{\int (3x^2 - 4x + 5) \, dx = x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x + C}\]

Evaluating a definite integral

\(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx = F(b) - F(a)\), where \(F\) is any antiderivative of \(f\), i.e. \(F'(x) = f(x)\).
Worked exampleEvaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{1}^{3} (2x + 1) \, dx\).
An antiderivative is \(F(x) = x^2 + x\)
\[F(3) = 3^2 + 3 = 12\]\[F(1) = 1^2 + 1 = 2\]\[\int_{1}^{3} (2x + 1) \, dx = F(3) - F(1) = 12 - 2\]\[\boxed{\int_{1}^{3} (2x + 1) \, dx = 10}\]

Area under a curve using integration

If \(f(x) \ge 0\) for \(a \le x \le b\), the area enclosed between the curve \(y = f(x)\), the \(x\)-axis, and the vertical lines \(x = a\) and \(x = b\) is given by \(\text{Area} = \displaystyle\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx\).
Worked exampleFind the area enclosed between the curve \(y = x^2 + 1\), the \(x\)-axis, and the vertical lines \(x = 1\) and \(x = 3\).
\[\text{Area} = \int_{1}^{3} (x^2 + 1) \, dx\]An antiderivative is \(F(x) = \dfrac{x^3}{3} + x\)
\[F(3) = \frac{27}{3} + 3 = 9 + 3 = 12\]\[F(1) = \frac{1}{3} + 1 = \frac{4}{3}\]\[\text{Area} = F(3) - F(1) = 12 - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{32}{3}\]\[\boxed{\text{Area} = \frac{32}{3} \approx 10.67 \text{ units}^2 \text{ (2 dp)}}\]
