Amplitude and principal axis (vertical shift) from maximum and minimum values

Amplitude \(a = \dfrac{\text{max} - \text{min}}{2}\), and the equation of the principal axis (vertical shift) is \(y = d\), where \(d = \dfrac{\text{max} + \text{min}}{2}\).
Worked exampleOver a single day, the highest temperature recorded is \(22^{\circ}\text{C}\) and the lowest is \(12^{\circ}\text{C}\). Find the amplitude and the vertical shift of a sinusoidal model for the temperature.
\[a = \frac{22 - 12}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5\]\[d = \frac{22 + 12}{2} = \frac{34}{2} = 17\]\[\boxed{a = 5, \ d = 17}\]

Period and the parameter b

For \(y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d\) or \(y = a \cos(b(x - c)) + d\), with \(x\) measured in degrees, the period is \(\dfrac{360}{b}\), so \(b = \dfrac{360}{\text{period}}\).
Worked exampleA Ferris wheel completes one full revolution every 8 minutes. Find the value of \(b\) for a sinusoidal model in degrees.
\[b = \frac{360}{\text{period}} = \frac{360}{8}\]\[\boxed{b = 45}\]

The general sinusoidal model y = a sin(b(x − c)) + d

\(y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d\), where \(a\) is the amplitude, \(b\) determines the period (period \(= \dfrac{360}{b}\) in degrees), \(c\) is the horizontal (phase) shift, and \(d\) is the vertical shift (principal axis \(y = d\)).
Worked exampleA Ferris wheel has minimum height 2 m (at \(t = 0\) minutes, at the boarding platform) and maximum height 32 m, completing one revolution every 8 minutes. The height is modelled by \(h(t) = 15 \sin(45(t - 2)) + 17\), where \(t\) is in minutes.
\[a = \frac{32 - 2}{2} = 15\]\[d = \frac{32 + 2}{2} = 17\]\[\text{Period} = \frac{360}{45} = 8 \text{ minutes}\]This matches the 8-minute rotation.
Predict the height after 5 minutes:
\[h(5) = 15 \sin(45(5 - 2)) + 17 = 15 \sin(135^{\circ}) + 17\]\[\sin(135^{\circ}) = 0.7071\]\[h(5) = 15(0.7071) + 17 = 10.6 + 17\]\[\boxed{h(5) \approx 27.6 \text{ m}}\]
