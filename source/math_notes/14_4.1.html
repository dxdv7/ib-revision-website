Distance between two points in 2D

d = √[(x2 − x1)2 + (y2 − y1)2], the distance between points A(x1, y1) and B(x2, y2).
Worked exampleFind the distance between A(4, −3) and B(−1.5, 5).
d = √[(−1.5 − 4)2 + (5 − (−3))2]
= √[(−5.5)2 + (8)2]
= √[30.25 + 64]
= √94.25
= 9.71 (3 sf)

Midpoint of two points in 2D

M = ((x1 + x2) / 2, (y1 + y2) / 2), the midpoint of the segment joining A(x1, y1) and B(x2, y2).
Worked examplePoints A(−3, 5) and B(0, 7) lie on the diameter of a circle. Find the coordinates of the centre.
The centre is the midpoint M of [AB].
M = ((−3 + 0) / 2, (5 + 7) / 2)
= (−1.5, 6)

Finding an unknown coordinate using the midpoint formula

If M(xM, yM) is the midpoint of A(x1, y1) and B(x2, y2), then xM = (x1 + x2) / 2 and yM = (y1 + y2) / 2 can each be solved for an unknown coordinate.
Worked exampleM(2, 4) is the midpoint of A(x, 1) and B(7, 7). Find x.
Using the x-coordinate formula: (x + 7) / 2 = 2
x + 7 = 4
x = −3
Check with the y-coordinate: (1 + 7) / 2 = 4, which matches yM = 4

Distance between two points in 3D

d = √[(x2 − x1)2 + (y2 − y1)2 + (z2 − z1)2], the distance between points A(x1, y1, z1) and B(x2, y2, z2).
Worked exampleA storage box has vertices including B(0, 3, 4) and R(2, 0, 0), all coordinates in metres. Find the length of diagonal BR, the maximum length of a tool that fits in the box.
BR = √[(2 − 0)2 + (0 − 3)2 + (0 − 4)2]
= √[4 + 9 + 16]
= √29
= 5.39 m (3 sf)

Midpoint of two points in 3D

M = ((x1 + x2) / 2, (y1 + y2) / 2, (z1 + z2) / 2), the midpoint of the segment joining A(x1, y1, z1) and B(x2, y2, z2).
Worked exampleA cuboid has a diagonal with endpoints A(4, 0, 0) and C1(0, 5, 2), coordinates in metres. Find the midpoint M of this diagonal.
M = ((4 + 0) / 2, (0 + 5) / 2, (0 + 2) / 2)
= (2, 2.5, 1)
