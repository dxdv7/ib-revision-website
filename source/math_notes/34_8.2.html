Expected frequency in a contingency table

\(E = \dfrac{\text{row total} \times \text{column total}}{\text{grand total}}\), where \(E\) is the expected frequency for a cell, assuming the two variables are independent.
Worked exampleIn a survey of 100 people, 50 are male and 50 are female. Overall, 45 people prefer tea and 55 prefer coffee.
Row total (male) = 50, column total (tea) = 45, grand total = 100.
\[\boxed{E(\text{male, tea}) = \frac{50 \times 45}{100} = 22.5}\]\[\boxed{E(\text{male, coffee}) = \frac{50 \times 55}{100} = 27.5}\]

The χ2 test statistic

\(\chi^2 = \displaystyle\sum \dfrac{(O-E)^2}{E}\) over all cells, where \(O\) is the observed frequency and \(E\) is the expected frequency of each cell.
Worked exampleA 2 by 2 table gives observed frequencies: Smokers with disease = 30, Smokers without disease = 20, Non-smokers with disease = 10, Non-smokers without disease = 40 (row totals 50 and 50, column totals 40 and 60, grand total 100).
\[E(\text{smoker, disease}) = \frac{50 \times 40}{100} = 20, \quad E(\text{smoker, no disease}) = \frac{50 \times 60}{100} = 30\]\[E(\text{non-smoker, disease}) = \frac{50 \times 40}{100} = 20, \quad E(\text{non-smoker, no disease}) = \frac{50 \times 60}{100} = 30\]\[\chi^2 = \frac{(30-20)^2}{20} + \frac{(20-30)^2}{30} + \frac{(10-20)^2}{20} + \frac{(40-30)^2}{30}\]\[\boxed{\chi^2 = 5 + 3.33 + 5 + 3.33 = 16.67}\]

Degrees of freedom

\(v = (r-1)(c-1)\), where \(r\) is the number of rows and \(c\) is the number of columns in the contingency table (not counting the totals row/column).
Worked exampleA contingency table has 4 rows (types of pet: dog, cat, bird, fish) and 3 columns (age groups).
\[\boxed{v = (4-1)(3-1) = 3 \times 2 = 6}\]

Reaching a conclusion: test statistic vs critical value, or p-value vs significance level

If \(\chi^2_{calc} > \text{critical value}\), reject \(H_0\); if \(\chi^2_{calc} \le \text{critical value}\), accept \(H_0\). Equivalently, if the p-value \(<\) significance level, reject \(H_0\); if the p-value \(\ge\) significance level, accept \(H_0\).
Worked exampleA \(\chi^2\) test for independence between favourite music genre and gender gives \(\chi^2_{calc} = 1.62\) with 3 degrees of freedom, and a p-value of 0.654. The test is carried out at the 1% significance level, with critical value 11.345.
\[\boxed{1.62 < 11.345 \ (0.654 > 0.01) \implies \text{accept } H_0}\]Favourite music genre is independent of gender.
