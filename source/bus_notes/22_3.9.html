3.9 — Budgets

Key terms

  • Cost center — a division of a business where costs can be isolated and recorded, but revenue cannot be clearly tracked or allocated (for example, IT, accounting, and human resources).
  • Profit center — a division of a business where both costs and revenues can be recorded and isolated, allowing profit to be calculated for that division (for example, the sales department).
  • Budget — a financial planning tool detailing an organization’s expected future income and expenditures over a particular period, used to set goals, communicate direction, and increase accountability and control.
  • Variance — the difference between an actual figure and its corresponding budgeted figure, calculated as the actual figure minus the budgeted figure.
  • Favorable variance (F) — a variance with a positive effect on profit: actual income greater than budgeted income, or actual expense less than budgeted expense.
  • Adverse variance (A) — a variance with a negative effect on profit: actual income less than budgeted income, or actual expense greater than budgeted expense.
  • Net income (in a budget) — total income minus total expenses within a budget, similar to the profit calculation on a profit and loss account.
