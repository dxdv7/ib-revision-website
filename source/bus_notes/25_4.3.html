4.3 — Sales forecasting

Key terms

  • Forecast — a description of what may happen in the future, based on analysis of past data.
  • Sales forecasting — the process of approximating future sales by analyzing historical sales data to identify an upward, downward, or constant trend.
  • Trend — the general pattern (upward, downward, or constant) shown by data over time.
  • Simple linear regression — a statistical method of forecasting in which actual sales are plotted against time and a straight trendline is drawn through the mean data point, following the general pattern of the data.
  • Mean data point — the point through which a trendline must pass, found by calculating the mean of the x-values (e.g. years) and the mean of the y-values (e.g. sales): mean = sum of data points ÷ number of data points.
  • Extrapolation — projecting a trendline beyond the range of the historical data used to create it, in order to forecast future values; the further the projection, the less accurate the forecast tends to be.
  • Moving average — a technique that smooths out short-term fluctuations in a data series by averaging a fixed number of consecutive periods, e.g. a three-year moving average for year 2 = (year 1 + year 2 + year 3) ÷ 3; used to reveal the underlying trend more clearly than the raw data.
  • Yearly variation — the difference between the actual data value and the moving average (trend) value for a given period: yearly variation = actual sales − trend.
  • Cyclical fluctuation — variation in sales data, appearing as peaks and troughs, most likely caused by wider external factors such as economic conditions.
  • Seasonal variation — fluctuation in sales caused by predictable seasonal factors (e.g. summer versus winter), which can also be smoothed out using a moving average to reveal a clearer underlying trend.
  • Trendline (line of best fit) — a straight line drawn through plotted data, passing through the mean data point, that represents the overall pattern of the data and can be extended to forecast future sales.
