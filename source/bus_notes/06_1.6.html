1.6 — Multinational companies (MNCs)

Key terms

  • Multinational company (MNC) — an organization that operates business activities in more than one country.
  • Host country — a country where a company based in another country (its home country) carries out business activities.
  • Globalization — the trend toward increasing expansion of large organizations' operations into more than one country, bringing multinational corporations into more communities worldwide.
  • Benefit of MNCs to host countries — MNCs can raise host-country GDP and provide capital investment, employment, training, higher wages, improved technology and infrastructure, and increased competition and product choice for local markets.
  • Drawback of MNCs to host countries — MNCs may show limited corporate social responsibility, deplete natural resources, worsen carbon emissions if environmental laws are relaxed for them, influence government policy, exploit local suppliers, or create a monopoly that drives local businesses out.
