3.1 — Introduction to finance

Key terms

  • Finance — the process of a business obtaining and managing the money needed to buy or do something for a particular purpose, and to meet the needs of society in a sustainable manner.
  • Capital expenditure — spending by a business on long-term or non-current assets used for more than one year, such as buildings, machinery, and equipment.
  • Revenue expenditure — the ongoing, short-term expenses (also called operating expenses) needed to run day-to-day business operations within the accounting period, such as purchasing raw materials/stock and paying wages and salaries.

Key theories & models

  • Triple bottom line (TBL/3BL) — a framework evaluating organizational performance across three pillars: people (social, fair treatment of stakeholders), planet (environmental, minimizing business impact), and profit (financial, achieved without compromising the other two pillars).
