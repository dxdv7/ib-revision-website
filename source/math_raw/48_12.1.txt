The line joining points A and B is called a chord.
The line through points A and B is called a secant.
The average rate of change from A to B is the same as 
the gradient of the line AB.
As B moves closer to A, the gradient of the line AB gets 
closer to the gradient of the tangent at A.
The gradient of a secant identifies the average rate of 
change while the gradient of a tangent identifies the 
instantaneous rate of change.

The gradient of a tangent is the limit of a secant.
The average rate of change between two points is the same as the gradient of 
the secant between the two points.
The instantaneous rate of change at a point is the gradient of the tangent at 
that point.
