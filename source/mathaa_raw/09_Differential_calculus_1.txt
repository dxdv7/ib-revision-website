Finding the gradient of a curve at a point - or better, finding a rule (function) 
that gives us the gradient at any point on the curve - is very useful information

represents the rate of change of y with respect to x. To find the gradient of a 
tangent, we first need to clarify what it means to say that a line is tangent to a 
curve at a point. Then we can establish a method to find the tangent at a point.
